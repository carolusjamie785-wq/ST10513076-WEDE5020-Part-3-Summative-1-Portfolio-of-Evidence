Our History

Founded in the 1970s-1980s as South Africa Big Box Warehouse retailer, Game Revolutionized Shopping with Discount Household goods, Electronics and groceries under one Roof.Game brief history, Game started in the 1970s to the 1980s as the first big box warehouse retail store in South Africa. The first store opened in Durban. The idea is simple sell household goods, furniture and groceries at low prices in a big warehouse with trolleys and bulk buying that was new for south Africa during the time.

During this game also expanded beyond just groceries and furniture they added electronics, TV and toys and later gaming consoles which today kids enjoy doing.
